## What was tested

A narrowing neck, not a declared topological transition. 0.08 was not an input.

Neumann grid dumbbell (chamber 8, neck length 5): lambda_1 falls from 0.0223 at width 8 to 0.00421 at width 1. Spectral collapse occurs. It occurs on a fixed topology with Neumann conditions. It does not prove a topological mouth.

The gap ratio lambda_1/lambda_2 is monotonic on that family (0.033 at w=1 to 0.251 at w=8) and crosses 0.08 between w=2 and w=3. That crossing is a level set. Change chamber size or neck length and it moves. It is not a zero of partial_{ln w}.

Dirichlet dumbbell and metric-graph bridge: no sign change in the tested scale betas. Conductance pinch has beta = 1 at every coupling.

## Non-claims

- Not a measurement of eta = 0.92.
- Not a Maxwell-stress integral.
- Not a universal informational counter-pressure constant.
